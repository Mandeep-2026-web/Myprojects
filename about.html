Our Story

Fifteen Years Around the Same Fire

Meet the Kitchen

Chef Marco Basile

Marco trained in the wood-fired kitchens of Tuscany before bringing the philosophy of "one flame, one pan" to Ember & Oak. He believes fire is the oldest seasoning — and the hardest to master.

Every cut on the menu passes through his hands: selected, aged, scored and finally set over embers he tends personally each evening.
